# Product Requirements Document — SADEEM | OUTPOST 72

## Product

**SADEEM | OUTPOST 72 — The Lost Message** is a cooperative Roblox STEM adventure developed for NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026.

Set in 2045, the game follows a Saudi astronaut crew exploring a mysterious space outpost on the Moon and Mars. Players restore essential systems, manage survival resources, solve puzzles, and uncover three hidden messages with guidance from NAJM, their robotic companion.

## Users and Goal

**Target Audience:** Children aged 7–12.

**Players:** 1–4 players, supporting solo and cooperative gameplay.

**Goal:** Teach young players how engineering decisions affect interconnected survival systems while encouraging problem-solving, teamwork, and STEM curiosity.

## Core Functional Requirements

- **FR1 — Tutorial:** Clear instructions, accessible controls, mission objectives, and readable feedback.
- **FR2 — Planetary Exploration:** Moon and Mars environments with location-specific educational content.
- **FR3 — Cooperative Multiplayer:** Host-controlled lobby for up to four players with synchronized mission progress.
- **FR4 — Outpost Systems:** Interactive equipment for power, oxygen, communications, and radiation protection.
- **FR5 — Mystery and Puzzles:** Three discoverable message fragments leading to a solvable final puzzle.
- **FR6 — Survival Challenge:** 72 simulated hours with resource indicators, hazards, and mission outcomes.
- **FR7 — Engineering Trade-offs:** Player decisions affect interconnected resources. Power loss disrupts other essential systems.
- **FR8 — NASA Scientific Content:** Archived NASA research and mission findings incorporated into educational gameplay.
- **FR9 — Mission Report:** A final summary of survival outcomes, scientific observations, and player decisions.
- **FR10 — NAJM Companion:** Interactive guidance throughout the mission.
- **FR11 — Accessibility:** Arabic and English support with age-appropriate instructions.

## NASA-Grounded Mechanics

SADEEM incorporates NASA scientific resources into its environmental challenges, educational puzzles, and exploration activities.

- **LRO / LOLA and Moon Trek:** Lunar terrain and exploration.
- **Mars Trek:** Martian terrain and landing-site references.
- **DONKI:** Solar hazards and space weather.
- **OSDR / RadLab:** Radiation and astronaut health.
- **NASA InSight:** Martian environmental conditions.
- **NASA Diviner:** Lunar surface temperatures.
- **NASA LCROSS:** Lunar water ice discovery.
- **NASA MOXIE:** Oxygen production on Mars.
- **Curiosity RAD:** Radiation protection.
- **JPL Horizons:** Planetary positioning.
- **NASA Planetary Fact Sheets:** Gravity and planetary information.
- **NASA Apollo Program:** Historical lunar exploration.

**Scientific Accuracy:** NASA research and historical findings are distinguished from fictional events. Resource consumption, environmental effects, and the 72-hour survival challenge use simplified educational simulations rather than actual NASA measurements.

All NASA references are documented in [`NASA_DATA_AND_SOURCES.md`](NASA_DATA_AND_SOURCES.md).

## Acceptance Tests

1. Players can start the game and complete the tutorial.
2. Moon and Mars selection provides different environments and educational content.
3. Resource decisions affect interconnected systems, including power and oxygen.
4. All three message fragments can be discovered and used to solve the final puzzle.
5. Players can respond to hazards through meaningful resource decisions.
6. Multiplayer participants share consistent mission progress.
7. The 72-hour survival challenge reaches a defined outcome.
8. Players can continue to the second destination.
9. The final report summarizes mission outcomes and decisions.
10. NASA-derived educational content identifies its scientific references.
