# NASA Data and Sources — SADEEM | OUTPOST 72

This document lists the official NASA scientific resources used to support SADEEM's educational gameplay, environmental simulations, and space exploration challenges.

> **SADEEM is an independent educational project and is not endorsed by NASA.** The story (Saudi crew, Outpost 72, 2045) is fictional. Survival resources and mission calculations are simplified gameplay values, not actual NASA measurements.

## Status Legend

- **Implemented (archived)** — Historical NASA research or scientific references incorporated into gameplay.
- **Planned** — Designed but not yet implemented or verified.

## Labeling Rules

1. NASA-derived information must reference its scientific source.
2. Historical findings are distinguished from simulated gameplay values.
3. Fictional events and survival calculations are not presented as NASA measurements.

## Scientific Accuracy

SADEEM incorporates NASA historical research, mission findings, and scientific references into educational gameplay. Environmental effects, resource consumption, and the 72-hour survival challenge are simplified simulations designed for learning and entertainment.
